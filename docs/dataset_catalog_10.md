# Model Lab: Ten-Dataset SME Catalog

The production catalog contains ten distinct canonical subject datasets. Each dataset is intended to support a narrow subject-matter-specialist model, while the datasets can also be combined into controlled composite corpora.

## Canonical datasets

| ID | Group | Primary specialization |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `swe_cs_systems` | Software engineering, computer science, systems engineering |
| 2 | `ai_ml_cybersec_dataeng` | AI/ML, defensive cybersecurity, data engineering |
| 3 | `sci_reasoning_forensics_formal` | Scientific reasoning, digital forensics, formal methods |
| 4 | `domain_finance_bio_robotics` | Quantitative finance, computational biology, robotics |
| 5 | `math_statistics_optimization` | Mathematics, statistics, probability, optimization |
| 6 | `physics_chemistry_materials` | Physics, chemistry, materials science |
| 7 | `biomedical_health_science` | Biomedical science, bioinformatics, health research |
| 8 | `law_compliance_governance` | Law, compliance, privacy, digital governance |
| 9 | `linguistics_information_retrieval` | Linguistics, NLP, search, information retrieval |
| 10 | `climate_energy_geospatial` | Climate science, energy systems, geospatial analysis |

## Composition families

These are combinations to evaluate as experiments, not predetermined model designs:

- Systems intelligence: 1 + 5
- AI systems research: 1 + 2 + 5
- Secure ML engineering: 1 + 2 + 3
- Scientific computing: 5 + 6
- Computational biology: 5 + 6 + 7
- Biomedical AI: 2 + 5 + 7
- Forensic engineering: 1 + 3 + 8
- Legal technology: 1 + 2 + 8
- Language infrastructure: 1 + 2 + 9
- Search and reasoning: 3 + 5 + 9
- Earth systems engineering: 1 + 5 + 6 + 10
- Climate and energy analytics: 5 + 6 + 10
- Robotics and physical intelligence: 1 + 5 + 6 + 10
- Quantitative science: 4 + 5 + 6
- Broad scientific engineering: 1 + 3 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 10

The pipeline must preserve each source group's identity when a composite run uses `--dataset-group all` or another explicitly selected group set. Composite experiments should therefore be treated as separate derived datasets rather than overwriting any canonical subject dataset.

## Provenance rule

Source manifests use schema 3; the independent lineage validator also reads schema 2 for backward compatibility. The canonical group definitions in `config/dataset_groups.yaml` are authoritative for source selection. `config/dataset_profiles.yaml` records the intended numeric dataset-to-group catalog and is also schema 2.

No runtime corpus, source manifest, tokenizer, shard, checkpoint, or GGUF artifact is committed to the repository. Those artifacts are generated by the pipeline after the catalog and provenance tests pass.
